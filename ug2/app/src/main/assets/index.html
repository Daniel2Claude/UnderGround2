Driving tips

  • Near misses fill the nitro tank faster than coasting does. Brush past traffic, do not wait for open road.
  • Nitro adds speed but widens your turning circle. Release it before a lane change.
  • Traffic speeds up with distance, and trucks block a lane for a long time. Plan your gap two cars ahead.
  • The Silvia turns in fastest, the Huracán is quickest in a straight line. Pick by how steady your hands are.
